# FIFO allocation of Sales to Lots is derived, never stored

Sales consume Lots of the same Asset in the same Envelope, oldest purchase first (FIFO). FIFO never crosses Envelopes: a Sale in a CTO never consumes a PEA Lot of the same Asset, which matches how brokers and the tax authority compute cost basis. The allocation, the remaining quantity of each Lot and the Realized P/L are recomputed from the full ordered history of each (Asset, Envelope) pair on every read, and no `remaining_quantity` column exists. Editing or deleting any Sale or Lot therefore cannot leave stale allocations, and an edit that would make a past Sale exceed the open quantity at its date is refused. We chose FIFO over average cost (PMP) to keep per-Lot P/L and the purchase-date FX split meaningful, at the price of recomputing history and of rewriting every Realized P/L figure if the method ever changes.
